AgriSmart Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

In short: AgriSmart uses the farm details you enter and your location to predict harvest timing and recommend fertilizer. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and you can ask us to delete your information at any time.

This policy explains how AgriSmart ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses and protects information when you use the AgriSmart mobile app for Android. AgriSmart is developed in Sierra Leone to help farmers plan harvests and use fertilizer well.

Information we collect

Farm information you enter. To give predictions and recommendations, the app asks for details such as crop type, planting date, farm or plot size, soil condition, target yield and fertilizer type.

Location. If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to find the weather for your farm. You can also turn location off and the app will ask you to choose your area instead. You can change this permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Technical information. When the app talks to our server, the server may automatically record basic technical details such as your IP address, the time of the request and error information. We use this only to keep the service working and to fix problems.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, messages, or payment information.

How we use your information

  • To predict when your crops will be ready for harvest.
  • To calculate fertilizer amounts for your crop and farm.
  • To get weather forecasts for your area.
  • To keep the app secure, fix errors and improve its accuracy.

Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share only what is needed to run the app:

  • Open-Meteo (weather service): receives your farm's approximate coordinates so it can return a weather forecast. It does not receive your name or farm details. See the Open-Meteo terms.
  • Our hosting provider: stores and runs the AgriSmart server on our behalf.
  • The law: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep information

We keep your farm information for as long as you use the app, so your predictions stay available. Technical server logs are deleted automatically after a limited period. If you ask us to delete your data, we will remove it within 30 days.

How we protect information

Information sent between the app and our server is encrypted using HTTPS. We limit access to the data to the people who run AgriSmart. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off location access in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • You can stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.

To make a request, email us at agrismartsierraleone@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". We will reply within 30 days.

Children

AgriSmart is made for farmers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will update the effective date at the top of this page, and for important changes we will let you know in the app.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your information, email agrismartsierraleone@gmail.com.
